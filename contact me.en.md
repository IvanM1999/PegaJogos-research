# 📧 Contact & Collaboration

Thank you for your interest in **PegaJogos-research**! This is a community effort to save a piece of Brazilian digital history. If you have files, technical knowledge, or memories about Pegajogo, your contribution is invaluable.

### 📫 How to get in touch
- **Email:** imperiobinario2@hotmail.com
- **Suggested Subject:** `[PegaJogos-research] Contribution - [Your Name/Subject]`

### 📂 Sending Files
To preserve data integrity and facilitate analysis, please follow these recommendations:
- **Small Files:** Can be attached directly to the email.
- **Large Files (Backups, ISOs, Databases):** Use cloud storage services (Google Drive, Dropbox, WeTransfer, Mega) and send the access link.
- **Files of Interest:** `.fdb`, `.gdb`, `.exe`, `.dll`, installers, screenshots, and manuals.

### 🤝 Ways to Contribute
1. **GitHub Issues:** For technical discussions, bug reports, or improvement suggestions.
2. **Direct Collaboration:** If you are a developer, malware/binary analyst, or Firebird DBA and want to participate actively, mention it in the email.
3. **History and Context:** Stories of how the software was used and old screenshots are essential for historical documentation.

### ⚖️ Privacy and Transparency
- **Privacy:** Your contact information and files will not be shared publicly without your explicit consent.
- **Credits:** All contributors will be cited in the acknowledgments section unless they prefer anonymity.
